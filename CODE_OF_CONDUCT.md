# Code of Conduct

This project follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short:

- Be respectful and considerate. Assume good intent.
- Welcome people of every background, identity, and level of experience,
  including people with disabilities and people who use assistive technology.
- Give and take feedback gracefully. Criticise ideas, not people.
- No harassment, insults, sexualised language, or publishing someone's private
  information.

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions, and anywhere else the
project is represented.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour is a problem, contact the maintainer privately through
GitHub at [@taylorarndt](https://github.com/taylorarndt). Reports are kept
confidential. The maintainer may remove comments, close issues, or block
accounts that break these rules.
